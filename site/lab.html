Forecast Lab. Grading itself in public.

Every 15 minutes during US market hours, a scheduled cloud job pulls fresh bars for 16 large-cap stocks, retrains, and publishes the probability that each one moves up over the next 5 minutes, the next hour and the next session. Every forecast is logged and scored once its bar closes. No cherry-picking.
