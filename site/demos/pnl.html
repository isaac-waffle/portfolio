Where do I get the CSV?
  1. Log in to DEGIRO web (trader.degiro.nl)
  2. Go to Activity → Transactions
  3. Set the period from your first trade until today
  4. Export → CSV
How it is calculated
  • Shares bought first are treated as sold first (FIFO).
  • P&L is realised on sale, including fees, in EUR.
  • Return is the % gain on the purchase cost of the shares sold.
  • Splits, mergers, dividends and FX gains are not included.
  • Your file is processed only in this browser and is never uploaded.
