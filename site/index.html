Live system

Forecast Lab. Grading itself in public.

Every 15 minutes during US market hours, a scheduled cloud job pulls fresh bars for 16 large-cap stocks, retrains, and publishes the probability that each one moves up over the next 5 minutes, the next hour and the next session. Every forecast is logged and scored once its bar closes. No cherry-picking.

Loading live data…

Hit rates are walk-forward: the model is retrained on a rolling window and scored only on bars it never saw. Research demo, not investment advice.

About

Rigour first. Then speed.

I care about the gap between a model that looks good and one that is good.

My training in econometrics taught me to ask the uncomfortable questions: is the effect causal, is the sample biased, does the edge survive costs, would it have worked without hindsight? I bring that discipline to fast, practical data work: pipelines that refresh on time, dashboards people actually use, and models whose limits are stated upfront.

Outside work I build tools for my own problems: a job radar for visa-sponsored roles, a P&L analyser for my brokerage account, a scam filter for the Amsterdam rental market. They are small products, and I build each one end to end.
